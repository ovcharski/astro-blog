Hi, I'm Nikola. I break software for a living, professionally and on purpose.

I'm a QA Automation Engineer. Over the years I've tested web apps, iOS and Android apps, and APIs, and these days I mostly make backend services sweat under load tests. Before that I reviewed laptops and phones, so poking at tech until something gives has been a long-running theme.

This blog is my playground: side projects, homelab tinkering, half-finished experiments, and the occasional thing that actually works.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm running. Usually too far, by choice.
